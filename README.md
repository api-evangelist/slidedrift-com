SlideDrift is a SaaS platform that helps professionals, founders, consultants, agencies, and teams turn notes, articles, and ideas into polished LinkedIn carousel posts. It provides tools for creating, editing, branding, exporting, and scheduling LinkedIn content, with AI integration and a visual content studio. Operated by Wu Wei Ltd, registered in Cyprus, SlideDrift offers public documentation, pricing, privacy terms, and support routes, enabling users to efficiently produce on‑brand LinkedIn posts at scale.

**Areas:** Company · SaaS · LinkedIn · ContentCreation · Automation
